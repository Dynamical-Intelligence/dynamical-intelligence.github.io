This project worked on an outreach pathway into AI with the goal of engaging students aged 8–18 through
sound and music. Music is a universal entry point that makes abstract AI concepts concrete, especially
for groups historically excluded from computing. We delivered culturally inclusive learning experiences across three phases:

- Accessible web platform: A browser-based interface for real-time analysis of pitch, rhythm, and stylistic
  features from user-supplied audio. [Explore the website](https://explorer.musicinformationretrieval.com/)

- Interactive tutorial modules: Five free Jupyter-notebook tutorials for A-level Computer Science, Physics,
  and Mathematics, guiding learners through data processing and machine learning with Python for text, image and audio. [Access the notebooks](https://github.com/musicinformationretrieval/epsrc-ai-pipeline)

- Educational magazine: A special issue of A Bit of CS4FN for ages 8–12, with accessible articles, visual storytelling, and hands-on activities that introduce core AI concepts through the analysis of art and cultural expression. [Read the magazine](https://cs4fn.blog/music-ai/)
