Today, **Sohyun Im** joined the Dynamical Intelligence Systems Lab at Queen Mary University of London, working on **Universal Acoustic Vision** as part of our ongoing collaboration with Meta.

The project aims to turn Meta's Project Aria smart glasses into a platform for acoustic imaging, using arrays of microphones to localize sound sources in real time. 

Welcome to the lab, Sohyun!
