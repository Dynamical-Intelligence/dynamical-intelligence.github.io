Franco Caspe is an Engineer, guitarist, and a PhD candidate at Queen Mary University of London. Franco walks the line between artificial intelligence for audio, real-time systems, and human–computer interaction. During his PhD, he explored the space between acoustic instruments and synthesizers, using neural networks for music performance applications.

As a creative practitioner, he has worked with several artists in the development of interactive installations and musical performances with generative AI. He was also an intern at Yamaha working on their Singing Voice System.

Currently, he is working as a postdoc developing Minifusion (https://minifusion.live), a novel AI platform for live instrumental sound transformation, and seeking to work on more adaptable tools that can help musicians and other art practitioners.
