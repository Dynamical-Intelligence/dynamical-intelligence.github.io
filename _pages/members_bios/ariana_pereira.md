After studying Finance at Montclair State University and spending more than 4 years on Wall Street working as a business strategist and financial analyst, Ariana transitioned into music technology research, and is pursuing a Master's in Sound and Music Computing at Universitat Pompeu Fabra.

Her research focuses on music perception, generative music systems, and computational approaches to designing personalized music technologies for health and wellbeing. Her current work spans emotionally conditioned music generation, deep learning approaches to analyzing the neural encoding of music, and the development of human-centered AI interfaces. 

Alongside her research, Ariana is an active performer, songwriter, and recording artist who has collaborated with bands and musicians throughout the New York City music scene. Her work is driven by a passion for exploring the therapeutic potential of music and sound, bridging artistic practice, scientific research, and technology to create more expressive, accessible, and impactful musical experiences.
