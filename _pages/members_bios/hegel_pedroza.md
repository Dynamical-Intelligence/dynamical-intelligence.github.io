Hegel Pedroza is a Professor-Researcher at Universidad Autónoma Metropolitana (UAM) Lerma, Mexico and a PhD student in Musical Technology at Universidad Nacional Autónoma de México. His research interests lie at the intersection of artificial intelligence, deep learning, music technology, and artistic research. He has published research on automatic guitar tablature transcription using machine learning and is currently working on deep learning approaches for real-time guitar transcription, as well as multimodal AI systems that integrate audio, video, and musical information. His work combines computational methods with music and multimedia art.
