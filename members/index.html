Irán Román

Lead Researcher · Lecturer

Queen Mary University of London

Iran R. Roman is a Lecturer at the School of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science of Queen Mary University of London. Within Queen Mary, he is a member of the Center for Multimodal AI, Center for Digital Music, Center for Human-Centered Computing, the Computer Vision group, and the Cognitive Science group.

His research area is machine perception, with the goal of creating algorithms that allow computers to perceive environments as living agents do. To this end, Iran has developed algorithms that leverage multimodal signals to sense, identify, and track objects in the real world. These algorithms draw inspiration from the neural mechanisms that allow living organisms to carry out similar tasks. Iran’s work has found applications in products at companies such as Apple, Tesla, Raytheon/BBN, and Plantronics. His research has been funded by the US National Science Foundation (NSF), the US Defense Advanced Research Projects Agency (DARPA), and the Howard Hughes Medical Institute (HHMI).

On the academic service side, he serves as reviewer for IEEE ICASSP, IEEE MLSP, ISMIR, eLife, and Music & Science. Iran is also a volunteer professor for the National Autonomous University of Mexico, and the organizer of the annual Deep Learning for Music Information Retrieval workshop at the Center for Computer Research in Music and Acoustics at Stanford University.

Lianganzi Wang

PhD Student

Queen Mary University of London

Lianganzi Wang is a PhD student at the School of Electronic Engineering and Computer Science, Queen Mary University of London, and a member of the Dynamical Intelligence Systems Lab at the Centre for Digital Music (C4DM). Her research centres on machine learning for generative audio: she studies how real measured contact forces can be used to condition neural audio generators, so that synthesized impact and contact sounds are physically realistic — with applications in Foley and sound design for film and games.

Before her PhD, she completed an MSc in Sound and Music Computing at QMUL, and spent an exchange year at University College Cork in Ireland, combining computer science with music technology. She is also a musician: an erhu player certified at Grade 10 by the China Conservatory of Music, and she enjoys creating her own music with AI tools.

Philipp Schmidt

MSc Student

Queen Mary University of London

Philipp Schmidt is an MSc Data Science student at Queen Mary University of London, where he is conducting his dissertation under the supervision of Dr Iran Roman at the Centre for Multimodal AI in close collaboration with Meta Reality Labs Research, Cambridge, UK. He holds a BSc in Mechanical Engineering and Management from the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology, Germany. During his undergraduate studies, he worked as a software developer and researcher in the Research and Development division of Dr. Ing. h.c. F. Porsche AG, Weissach, Germany, where he focused on vehicle diagnostics.

His research interests span distributed systems and machine learning. He develops distributed tracing and statistical correlation algorithms for diagnostics in distributed systems, alongside machine learning methods for multichannel audio, with applications in acoustic imaging and sound event localisation.

Jimena Arruti

Research Assistant · MSc Student

Queen Mary University of London

Jimena Arruti is currently pursuing her MSc in Sound and Music Computing and and working as a Research Assistant at the Centre for Digital Music, Queen Mary University of London. She holds an Electrical Engineering BSc from Universidad de la República (Uruguay), focused on Signal Processing and Machine learning. She has previously worked in industry as an R&D Engineer, and in education as a Lecturer and teaching assistant. She is also a composer and musician, and she focus her research on creative tools for music.

During her time at the Dynamical Intelligence Systems Lab, she has collaborated with the EPSRC AI through music project, creating teaching materials for high school students to learn Artificial Intelligence concepts through musical applications. She is also developing the lab’s website.
